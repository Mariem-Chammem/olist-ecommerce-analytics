The business:

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace connecting sellers to customers.

The business process:

Think about the lifecycle:
Customer
   ↓
Places order
   ↓
Order approved
   ↓
Payment
   ↓
Seller processes order
   ↓
Carrier
   ↓
Delivery
   ↓
Customer receives product
   ↓
Customer reviews purchase

Analytical Domains:

Domain 1 — Sales
Questions:
How much is being sold?
How are sales evolving over time?
Which categories drive sales?
Which products drive sales?

Domain 2 — Customers
Questions:
Who are the customers?
How frequently do they purchase?
How valuable are they?
Are customers returning?
Which customer segments exist?

Domain 3 — Products
Questions:
Which products/categories perform best?
Which products sell frequently?
Are expensive products necessarily more valuable?

Domain 4 — Operations
Questions:
How long does delivery take?
Are orders delivered on time?
Which geographic areas experience delays?

Domain 5 — Customer Experience
Questions:
What is customer satisfaction?
Does delivery performance affect reviews?
Which categories receive poor reviews?

Domain 6 — Geography
Questions:
Where are customers concentrated?
Where are sellers concentrated?
Which states generate the most sales?
Are some regions operationally problematic?

Domain 7 — Customer Intelligence
Questions:
Who are the most valuable customers?
Which customers are at risk?
Can we segment customers based on behavior?

Main business problem:
How can an e-commerce marketplace use transactional, customer, product, operational, and external data to understand its performance, identify customer and product opportunities, and improve decision-making?
