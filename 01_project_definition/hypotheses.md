Customers receiving orders later than expected tend to give lower review scores.

H2 — Customer value

A relatively small proportion of customers contributes a disproportionately large share of marketplace revenue.

H3 — Product performance

Product categories with higher order volumes do not necessarily generate the highest average order value.

H4 — Geography

Sales performance varies substantially across Brazilian states.

H5 — Customer behavior

Customers can be meaningfully differentiated based on recency, frequency, and monetary value.

H6 — Customer experience
